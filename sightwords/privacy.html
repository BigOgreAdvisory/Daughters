Sight Word Quest: Privacy Policy

Sight Word Quest collects no personal information.

  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app makes no network requests while you play. After the first load it works offline.
  • The child's name and progress are saved only on the device (in the app's local storage). We never receive them. Clearing the app's data erases them.
  • The optional "Record me" button uses the microphone for three seconds so the child can hear their own voice. The recording is played back once and is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • The voice that reads words aloud is the device's own text-to-speech.
